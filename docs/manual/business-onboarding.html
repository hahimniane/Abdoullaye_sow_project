1 · Before you start

Laawol connects customers who want to ship barrels and freight, buy vehicles, move cars, or park them, with the businesses that provide those services. You work from the business console in a web browser. Your customers use the Laawol mobile app or the customer website.

Have these ready before you begin:

  • The email address you want to sign in with — it becomes the owner account
  • Your business licence or authority documents as PDFs or photos (up to 20 MB each)
  • Your bank details, for the payout account you'll connect
  • The countries you serve and the prices you want to charge

Work through sections 4 to 9 in order. Each one depends on the one before it: you can't take orders until you're approved, and you can't be paid until your payout account is connected.

2 · Applying to join

You apply once. Until a Laawol administrator approves you, customers can't see your business — so the application is the gate to everything else in this guide.

There are two ways in, and they create the same application:

WhereHow to get there
The website Go to laawoldigital.com and choose List your business
The mobile app On the sign-in screen, choose Register your business

The owner account

The first section creates the login you'll use to manage the business. Use an address you'll keep — it becomes the owner account, and the owner is the only role that can later invite staff or change payout settings.

Services and business profile

Next you tick the services you can provide and describe the business itself. Everything here is what the platform reviews, so the Tell us about your service box is worth filling in properly — routes, pricing, and experience all help your application through.

Missed a service? Whatever you tick here is only a starting point — once approved, you can turn services on or off any time from Services & coverage.

Finally you accept the terms and confirm you understand that your business — not Laawol — is the party providing the service to the customer. Submit, and the application goes to a Laawol administrator for review.

Applying from the app

The app's form asks for the same three things in the same order, and adds a business profile picture — a logo or storefront image customers will recognise.

3 · Signing in

Go to business.laawoldigital.com and sign in with your business email. You'll land on Today, your daily summary. Everything else is reached from the sidebar on the left, grouped into Business setup, Vehicle sales, Transport & shipping, and Manage.

The FR button in the bottom-right corner switches the whole console to French. Anything you type stays as you typed it.

4 · STEP 1Business identity and verification

Open Business under Business setup. This is where you tell Laawol who you are and prove you're allowed to trade.

Fill in your legal name, address, and contact details, then upload the documents for the services you intend to offer. You only need the documents that apply to you:

DocumentNeeded if you offer
Freight or shipping authority Barrel shipping, freight
Dealer licence or sales authorisationCar sales
Transport insurance and authorityCar transport
Parking facility proofCar parking

Each file can be up to 20 MB. Once uploaded, a document sits at submitted until a Laawol administrator reviews it and marks it verified.

Approval has two requirements, and documents are only one of them: your Stripe payout account (section 8) must also have finished verification. An administrator physically cannot approve a business whose Stripe setup is incomplete — so if approval seems slow, check the Stripe banner on Today first. Once both are done, the header next to your name changes from Pending to Approved.

Until you're approved, customers can't find you. You can configure everything else in the meantime, but your services won't appear to customers and you can't receive orders.

5 · STEP 2Choosing your services

Open Services & coverage. Turn on only the services you actually provide — each one you enable adds setup work and exposes you to orders you'd have to fulfil.

ServiceWhat it means for you
Barrel shipping Customers send full barrels to a destination country you serve
Freight Parcel shipping priced by weight, by air or by sea
Car sales You list vehicles; customers reserve them with a paid hold
Car transport You bid to move a customer's vehicle overseas
Car parking You store vehicles at your facility for a daily or monthly rate

Selecting a service reveals only the settings it needs, under Service rules — for example paid-hold pricing for car sales, or whether you collect freight from a customer's address. A service shows Not offered until you turn it on.

The two rules you are most likely to change:

RuleWhat it controls
Car sales · paid holds What a customer pays to reserve a vehicle, either a flat fee or a per-day rate, and how many days the hold survives before it lapses
Freight · customer pickup With pickup off, customers bring freight to one of your offices. Turn it on to collect from their address and price the collection

6 · STEP 3Your office locations

Anyone who chooses "bring it to the office" picks from this list, so add every branch a customer could physically walk into. A missing branch is a branch no customer can select.

Use Pause rather than deleting when a branch closes temporarily. Pausing hides it from customers while keeping it attached to the shipments already booked against it; deleting it breaks that link.

7 · STEP 4Destinations and pricing

Still under Services & coverage, add each country you serve and set your prices per service. Coverage is per service, per country — you might ship barrels to Guinea and Senegal but only offer freight to Senegal.

For each destination you set:

  • Barrel shipping — price per barrel and a delivery estimate range in days
  • Freight — price per kilogram for air and for sea, plus the departure days you sail or fly
  • Car transport — whether you cover that country at all

A price of zero means "not offered". If you leave the sea freight rate at zero, customers won't be offered sea freight to that country — the service simply won't appear for them.

8 · STEP 5Getting paid

Laawol collects payment from the customer and passes your share to you. To receive money you must connect a payout account, which you'll be prompted to do from your dashboard. Until payouts are enabled, orders can still arrive but money can't reach you.

Three numbers govern every transaction:

TermWhat it is
Gross receivedWhat the customer paid in total
Platform fee Laawol's commission, set per business and shown on your dashboard
Business earningsWhat reaches you after the platform fee

There's also a setting for who absorbs the card processing fee — you or Laawol. If you absorb it, payments for your orders are charged directly on your connected account and the processing fee comes out of your share. Your dashboard states which applies under Who pays Stripe's processing fee.

Connecting your Stripe account — the walkthrough

Laawol pays you through Stripe. On a brand-new business, Stripe setup is the very first thing your dashboard asks for — a banner marked Required first step sits above everything else until it's done. Orders can still arrive before then, but no money can reach you, and your business cannot be approved until Stripe verification is complete.

Choose Start Stripe registration. Stripe opens in the same tab and walks you through a short series of screens. Every screen below is from a real run of this flow.

1 · Contact details. Your email and mobile number — Stripe texts a verification code to that number, so use a phone you have with you.

2 · Business type. If you haven't filed paperwork to register a company, pick Individual — Stripe's own guidance on the page says the same.

3 · Personal details. Your legal name exactly as it appears on government records, date of birth, home address, and the last four digits of your Social Security number.

4 · Business details. Your industry and website. If you don't have a website, choose Add product description instead and describe what you sell.

5 · Bank account. The account your earnings are paid into — routing and account number, US accounts only.

6 · Identity document. If Stripe can't confirm your name and SSN automatically, it asks for a photo ID. You can do it on the spot with your phone camera, or Skip for now and finish later — but payouts stay blocked until it's done.

Stripe may not finish in one sitting. If you leave anything incomplete, the console banner changes to Finish Stripe setup to continue and the button becomes Continue Stripe setup, which reopens Stripe exactly where you left off. Your account isn't fully live until both Payouts enabled and Charges verified show green.

What approval looks like

Once Stripe verification is complete and Laawol has reviewed your documents, an administrator approves the business. Your dashboard header changes from Pending to Approved — from that moment customers can see your business and place orders.

You give your bank details to Stripe, not to Laawol. Laawol never sees or stores your bank account or card details — the console only ever shows the status Stripe reports back, plus your account reference.

9 · STEP 6Your team

Open People to invite staff. Each person gets their own sign-in and only sees the sections you allow.

Inviting someone

Choose Invite staff, enter their name and email, then tick the sections they should be able to manage before sending.

You never set or see their password. They receive an email invitation and set their own, and that invitation expires — if they leave it too long, invite them again rather than trying to reset it.

Permissions are per section: Business, Listings, Purchases, Barrels, Freight, Transport, Parking, Services & coverage, People, Reviews, Support, and Growth. Tick only what that person needs and press Save permissions. A service you don't offer shows as not yet offered and can't be granted.

The owner always has full access. Staff permissions apply to everyone else, so give warehouse staff the shipping queues without exposing pricing or payouts.

10 · Today — your daily view

Today is where you start each morning. It answers one question: what needs attention right now.

  • Counters across the top — active listings, open shipments, pending purchases, support requests
  • Operations mix — every order you have, grouped by status, so a growing "Pending" column tells you work is piling up
  • Business earnings — gross, fees, your earnings, and money still pending, broken down per service
  • Needs attention — the specific orders waiting on you

11 · Vehicle sales

Listings

Listings holds the vehicles you're selling. Use New listing to add one — make, model, year, condition, mileage, price, and photos. Each listing carries a rebuilt title status, which customers see before they reserve.

A listing's status moves draft → active → reserved → sold. Only active listings appear to customers. Use Export CSV to take your inventory into a spreadsheet.

Purchases

Purchases shows customers who have paid a hold deposit to reserve a vehicle. Review each one and either confirm the sale or release the hold. Deposits appear under hold deposits on your dashboard until resolved.

12 · Barrels and freight

These are your shipping queues. Both work the same way: a customer books and pays, the order lands in your queue, and you move it through its statuses as the goods travel.

Every shipment shows its tracking code, sender and receiver, destination, what was paid, and the delivery estimate the customer was quoted. Change Update status as the shipment progresses. The customer sees that change in their app immediately.

Freight is priced by weight. If the actual weight differs from what the customer declared, the difference is settled as a balance payment before the shipment can move on.

13 · Car transport

Car transport is a bidding marketplace, not first-come first-served. When a customer requests a vehicle move to a country you cover, the request appears in your Transport queue as an opportunity.

  1. A request arrives and you're notified
  2. You send a quote — total price, estimated pickup and delivery dates, transport method, and your terms
  3. The customer chooses which quote to accept. You don't claim the job; you win it on price and terms
  4. Once selected, it becomes a job you move through pending → scheduled → in transit → delivered

You can revise or withdraw a quote while the customer is still deciding. Each opportunity has a quote deadline — miss it and the opportunity closes.

"Scheduled" means booked, not moving. Use it once you've assigned a carrier and fixed a pickup date but the vehicle hasn't left. If that stage doesn't match how you work, go straight to in transit.

14 · Container tracking

Anything travelling in a container — barrels, sea freight, and cars — needs a container number before you can mark it in transit.

On each shipment card, find Automated container tracking. Enter the container, booking, or bill of lading number from your carrier, pick the carrier from the list, and press Start tracking. From then on, carrier milestones flow into the shipment automatically and your customer can follow it.

The system will not let you mark a load "in transit" without it. This is deliberate: in transit is the moment your customer starts asking where their goods are, and the container number is the only thing that can answer them. Air freight is exempt, since it has no container.

Pick your carrier from the list rather than typing its code. A mistyped carrier code doesn't fail loudly — tracking simply never starts. If your carrier isn't listed, choose Another carrier and enter its four-letter SCAC code.

15 · Notifications

The bell in the top-right tells you when something needs you. You'll be notified when:

  • A customer pays for any of your services — barrels, freight, car sales, parking, or transport
  • A new transport request arrives that you're eligible to quote on
  • A customer messages you through support
  • Your verification is reviewed

Clicking a notification takes you straight to the order it refers to, not just to the section. You can also receive these as push notifications on your phone and by email; adjust that under your notification preferences.

16 · Reviews, support, and growth

Reviews

Customers rate you after a completed order. Your average rating and review count appear to customers when they're choosing a business, so this directly affects how much work you win. You can report a review you believe is abusive.

Support

Support is your conversation with customers about specific orders. Each case is attached to the order it concerns, so you can see what they're referring to without asking.

Growth

Growth shows which plan you're on and what changes if you move. Every operational tool in this guide — listings, destinations, barrels, purchases, parking, transport, people, and support — is on the Free plan. Nothing in your day-to-day work is behind a paywall.

Pro adds three things on top:

  • The business AI advisor — it reviews your pricing, listings, and response times and tells you what's costing you work
  • Deeper analytics beyond the totals on Today
  • Reduced platform fees as your volume grows, so more of each order reaches you

On Free, Generate recommendations is visible but the panel below stays empty — that's the advisor waiting on an upgrade, not a fault.

17 · What your customers see

Your customers use the Laawol mobile app. Knowing what they see helps you understand what they're describing when they contact you.

The status you set in your queue is the status they see here. When you move a shipment to in transit, their Activity list updates immediately — which is why keeping statuses current cuts down on "where is my order" messages.
