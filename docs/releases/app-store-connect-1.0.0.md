### Description

Laawol brings trusted service businesses and customers together in one clear,
trackable marketplace.

Use Laawol to:

- Find registered businesses and review their service details.
- Request barrel shipping, shared barrels, freight, and vehicle transport.
- Browse vehicle listings, review title information, and reserve a viewing.
- Request sourcing and other professional services.
- Review prices and service details before confirming.
- Pay through secure payment processing and keep clear receipts.
- Track orders, shipments, refunds, and support conversations in one place.

Businesses can manage their profiles, destinations, listings, team access, and
service operations through Laawol’s connected business tools.

Service availability, destinations, pricing, and timing are set by each
registered provider and may vary by location. Laawol keeps the request,
payment status, tracking history, and support record organized from start to
finish.

### Review notes

Laawol is a marketplace for physical services and goods. Payments in this app
are for real-world shipping, vehicle, sourcing, parking, and related services;
the app does not sell digital content or unlock digital functionality.

Reviewer path:

1. Sign in with the review credentials.
2. Home shows the available marketplace services.
3. Shipping contains barrel, shared-barrel, freight, and vehicle-transport
   requests.
4. Cars contains vehicle listings and viewing reservations. Vehicle listings
   disclose whether a title is rebuilt.
5. Activity contains orders and tracking.
6. Settings contains Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, support, and the
   in-app account deletion flow at Settings → Account management → Delete
   account. Account deletion requires password reauthentication and explains
   the 30-day completion period and legally required record retention.

No payment needs to be completed during review. Use the provided fictional
data to inspect service details and tracking without creating a real shipment.
