Text messages

Text message terms

How Laawol and the shipping businesses that use it send you text messages about a package, how you agree to them, and how to stop them.

Effective: November 23, 2026

In short

We text you only about a package you dropped off, and only when you ask for it.

  • One message per request. No marketing.
  • Reply STOP to stop, HELP for help.
  • We never share your mobile number or your consent for marketing.
Contact us

1. Who sends these messages

Laawol Digital LLC (“Laawol”) operates the Laawol platform. Shipping businesses that use Laawol to register packages going from the United States to West Africa send package messages through Laawol’s toll-free number, +1 (866) 346-6017. Each message starts with the name of the business where you dropped off your package.

2. How you agree to receive a text

You agree in person, at the counter. When you drop off a package, the staff member asks for your mobile number and asks:

“We can text your package receipt to this number: one message each time you ask for a receipt. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to stop at any time. Our terms and privacy policy are at laawoldigital.com/sms-en.html. Would you like your receipt by text message, yes or no?”

If you say yes, the staff member answers: “Thank you. I am sending it now; the text you receive is your confirmation.” The staff member presses “Send receipt” and chooses “Text message” only when you say yes. If you say no, no text is sent. The same question is shown to the staff member on the screen before a text can be sent. Agreeing is not a condition of shipping a package: you can have your receipt on paper, by email or on WhatsApp instead.

3. What we send

One text message with a link to your package receipt (the price, what you have paid and what is left to pay). For example:

Example Shipping LLC: your package receipt CL-K7M4P2 / votre reçu de colis CL-K7M4P2 : https://laawoldigital.com/d?… Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

We do not send advertising or promotions by text message.

4. How often

One message each time you ask for a receipt. There are no recurring or scheduled messages.

5. Cost

Laawol and the shipping business charge nothing for these messages. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

6. How to stop, and how to get help

Reply STOP to any message to stop all text messages from this number. You will get one last message confirming it: “You have successfully been unsubscribed. You will not receive any more messages from this number. Reply START to resubscribe.” Reply START to receive messages again.

Reply HELP for help, or write to legal@laawoldigital.com. The reply to HELP is: “Laawol Digital: package receipts by text. Help: legal@laawoldigital.com. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop.”

7. Your number and your privacy

We use your mobile number only to send the message you asked for. We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or your text message consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy.

8. Delivery

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. If a text does not reach you, the business can show you the receipt, print it or email it.
