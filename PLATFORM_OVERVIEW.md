# Laawol Digital Platform Overview

## The Simple Idea

Laawol Digital is a marketplace platform for services that many diaspora customers already need: shipping barrels, buying cars, transporting cars, parking or storing cars, sourcing items from home, and getting help when something goes wrong.

The most important point is this:

Laawol is not trying to look like it personally does every service.

Laawol is the platform. Registered businesses provide the services. Customers use Laawol to find those businesses, compare details, pay, track progress, and get support.

That makes the platform useful for both sides. Customers get a clearer and safer experience. Good businesses get a more organized way to reach customers and manage their work.

## Why This Matters

Many people already use informal networks for shipping, cars, food, sourcing, and professional services. That can work, but it often creates confusion:

- Customers do not always know which business is responsible.
- Prices and delivery details are not always clear.
- Photos, receipts, tracking numbers, and payment history can be scattered.
- Refunds can be hard to understand.
- Support teams may not have enough information to help quickly.
- Good businesses can be hidden behind disorganized communication.

Laawol is being built to organize this.

The platform should make it easier to see who is providing the service, what the customer paid for, what the current status is, and who needs to take action next.

## What Exists Today

There are four main parts in place today:

- A customer mobile app.
- Business and staff tools inside the mobile app.
- An admin website for the platform team.
- A public website that explains the platform to customers and businesses.

These parts are connected around the same idea: businesses are the service providers, customers make requests or purchases, and the platform keeps the records organized.

## Customer Mobile App

The mobile app already supports customer accounts, sign in, sign up, profile editing, profile photos, phone information, notification preferences, language settings, dark mode, and account security options.

For car shopping, customers can browse active car listings from approved businesses. Listings use the real photos attached to each car. Customers can search, filter, sort, save favorites, open a car details page, contact the business, schedule a viewing, place a paid hold, request more hold time, and complete a car purchase.

For barrel shipping, customers can choose a destination country, see which approved businesses ship there, compare the business price, delivery estimate, and destination details, then submit a shipment request. The flow supports pickup or office drop-off, sender and receiver details, destination-specific phone validation, wallet credit use, card payment, receipts, and shipment tracking.

Customers can also view their purchases, track shipments, see wallet information, and request that wallet money be returned to their card.

## Business And Staff Tools

Businesses can apply to join the platform from the mobile app. During registration, they can add owner information, business contact details, a profile image, service notes, and the services they want to offer.

Each business can manage its profile, contact information, address, profile photo, enabled services, and car hold pricing.

For shipping, each business can decide which countries it serves. A business can turn a country on or off, set the barrel shipping price, add delivery time estimates, and write destination details for customers. This is important because destinations are not global for every business. Each business controls its own routes and terms.

Business owners can also add staff under their business. Staff and business owners are meant to stay connected to the business they work for, instead of being mixed into one flat user list.

The staff dashboard shows work connected to the business, including barrel shipments, transport requests, parked cars, and recent activity. Platform admins can see across businesses, while normal business users are limited to their own business.

## Admin Website

The admin website is the command center for the platform team.

The Businesses area is designed to be the main support workspace. Admins can add a business, see its status, update the status, and view the business owner, staff, services, listings, payments, support records, and related customer activity.

Business statuses include pending, approved, suspended, changes requested, and rejected.

Admins can assign an existing account to a business as the owner or staff. Only one owner is allowed for a business. If the owner is replaced, the previous owner becomes staff. A business owner or staff member must belong to a real business.

The People area separates platform admins, customer accounts, business-linked people, and contact-only support references. This helps avoid the old problem where every person was scattered in one list. Contact-only records are kept for support, but they are not treated like real customer accounts unless they have an actual account.

The Marketplace area groups car listings by business. Admins can see listing photos, filter by business, status, location, price, and search terms, then change listing status when needed. A halted listing is handled as an inactive listing. Listings can also be reserved or sold.

The Operations area lets admins review service records such as barrel shipments, car transport, car parking, and car purchases. Barrel shipment records include the sender, receiver, phone numbers, destination, payment information, tracking code, business, and status history.

The Finance area gives the platform team a wider view of money movement across businesses. It brings together wallet activity, refund requests, shipments, transport records, parking records, car purchases, and sold or reserved listings. Admins can review refund requests and check the customer account before completing or rejecting a return to card.

The admin website also includes support messaging. Customer support can send a request or message to a business when a customer needs help, when a payment needs review, or when an order needs attention.

## Admin Roles And Accountability

The platform supports different administrator roles, including super admin, operations manager, finance manager, and support admin.

Super admins have the widest access. They can manage businesses, people, roles, listings, operations, finance, and platform settings.

Other admin roles can be limited by responsibility. For example, finance admins can focus on money and refunds. Support admins can focus on customer and business support. Operations admins can focus on businesses, marketplace listings, and service records.

The admin website also has a role and privilege management area where platform roles can be adjusted.

Important admin actions are recorded for accountability. Status changes, business creation, business membership changes, owner replacement, role changes, refund reviews, and support messages create audit records. This matters because the platform needs to know who changed what and when.

## Public Website

The public website now presents Laawol as a platform where registered businesses provide services.

The message is clearer:

- Businesses register on the platform.
- Businesses provide the services.
- Customers compare services before choosing.
- Laawol organizes pricing, destinations, listings, payments, tracking, support, and accountability.

This is the right direction because customers should not think Laawol personally owns every service. They should understand that Laawol helps them find and work with registered businesses in a more trusted way.

## What Makes The Platform Valuable

For customers, Laawol should make it easier to answer:

- Which business is handling my request?
- What did I pay?
- What is the status?
- Where is my shipment, car, or order?
- Who should I contact?
- Can support see enough information to help me?
- If money is owed back, what is happening with it?

For businesses, Laawol should make it easier to:

- Publish services clearly.
- Show prices, destinations, and listing details.
- Manage staff under one business.
- Receive customer requests in an organized way.
- Build trust through clear records and good service.
- Work with platform support when a customer needs help.

For the platform team, Laawol should make it easier to:

- See everything connected to a business.
- Help customers without guessing.
- Review payments and refunds.
- Control business and listing status.
- Manage admin roles.
- Keep accountability records.

## Services Already Represented

The current platform already has work in place for:

- Barrel shipping.
- Business-specific shipping destinations.
- Car listings from businesses.
- Car photos, favorites, viewing reservations, paid holds, and purchases.
- Car transport records.
- Car parking or storage records.
- Customer wallets and refund requests.
- Business applications and approvals.
- Business staff management.
- Admin support messages to businesses.
- Admin role and privilege management.

Some future services, such as event tickets, sourcing, food, and professional services, fit naturally into this same model because they also need registered providers, clear pricing, customer records, payment history, and support.

## Current Readiness

The project has a strong working foundation, but it should not be treated as fully production-ready until final launch checks are completed.

The strongest parts today are the platform structure, business-centered admin console, mobile customer flows, business destination management, listing photos and filters, support visibility, finance visibility, refund review flow, and role-based admin access.

Before a real launch, the platform still needs final production review around:

- Live payment setup instead of simulated or test payment behavior.
- End-to-end testing of customer, business, admin, payment, refund, and support flows.
- Clear refund, cancellation, and paid hold policies.
- Legal pages such as terms, privacy policy, and refund policy.
- Business onboarding rules and approval standards.
- Monitoring and support procedures.
- Final review of security rules, admin privileges, and deployment settings.

## Plain Summary

Laawol Digital is being built as a trusted service marketplace.

Customers use it to find registered businesses, compare details, pay, track progress, and get help.

Businesses use it to publish their services, manage staff, list cars, set shipping destinations, handle customer work, and build trust.

Admins use it to see the whole picture, support customers, manage businesses, review money movement, handle refunds, control statuses, and keep accountability records.

The platform is not just a website or an app. It is the operating system for connecting customers, businesses, payments, tracking, and support in one organized place.
