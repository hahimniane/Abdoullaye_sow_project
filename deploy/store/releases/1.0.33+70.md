## What it carries

- The Fleet driver's own account (docs/FLEET.md section 11): Home (what is
  owed, how to pay by Zelle, "Send my payment screenshot", reported
  payments, last payments), Payments, My plate, and Settings without the
  marketplace rows. It stands where the customer home would when the
  business shows its drivers Fleet alone; otherwise the regular home gets a
  "Your plate" card.
- The fleet business's own shipping for a locked driver (section 13): Send a
  package (the request form pinned to that business) and My packages (that
  business's only).
- A push about the driver's account makes the screens read it again
  (section 12).
