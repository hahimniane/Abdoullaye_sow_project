## What it carries

- Waiting packages: register before a container exists, price and payments with
  who received the money, tick-list loading with the destination-country block,
  send back to waiting, labels with both phone numbers.
- Destinations screen: only countries in use, main-destination star, a
  confirmation before seeding the whole catalog.
- Inventory (off until a platform admin switches the service on): products,
  stock, 2x1 in QR tags, Sell screen (scan, basket, price override, payment),
  sales with void, today summary. Online only.
- The customer app no longer errors on the new public stage waiting_container.
