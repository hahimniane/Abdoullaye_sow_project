# Models used by the 3D box designer

Both files were optimized from the originals (all but one pose kept, the left
shoe removed, geometry quantized and meshopt-compressed with glTF-Transform);
the artwork itself is unchanged.

## man.glb - the 5 ft 6 in person

- Model: "Man" (low-poly adult male, standing pose)
- Author: Quaternius (https://quaternius.com/)
- Source: https://poly.pizza/m/HMnuH5geEG
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Attribution is not required; recorded here anyway.
